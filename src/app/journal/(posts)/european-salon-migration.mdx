**Eddie's Cut** is a hair salon in Hellerup, Denmark, with more than 25 years of experience. Their reputation was excellent. Their website was not keeping up.

### The problem: slow, dated and risky to move

The salon's website ran on a site builder. On a phone it was slow: Google PageSpeed gave it **47 out of 100** for mobile performance. The design also looked older than the salon itself, and booking was clumsy.

Moving away was risky too. Their business email ran through their existing host (One.com), so a careless move could cut off client emails.

### What we did

1. **A new website in Next.js.** We designed and built the site from scratch, with pages generated ahead of time so they load fast on phones.
2. **Small touches that feel premium.** A smooth menu that follows you as you scroll, clear prices, and booking buttons where people look for them.
3. **A safe hosting move.** We pointed the website to its new host step by step and kept the email (MX) records pointing at One.com, so the site never went down and email kept working.

### The result

On the same Google Lighthouse mobile test, the new site scores:

- **Performance: 92** (was 47)
- **Accessibility: 100** (was 88)
- **Best Practices: 100**
- **SEO: 100** (was 92)

The salon now has a website that loads quickly on a phone and looks like the business they have spent 25 years building. You can see the before and after screenshots in the [Eddie's Cut case study](/playroom/eddies-cut/).
