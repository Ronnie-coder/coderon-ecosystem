An agency's true test isn't just building something that looks good—it's engineering a platform that performs flawlessly in the real world. Recently, Coderon partnered with **Eddie's Cut**, a premium hair salon based in Hellerup, Denmark, with over 25 years of high-end hairdressing experience. 

They had the offline reputation, but their online presence was actively hurting their business. Here is how we transformed their digital footprint.

### The Challenge: Outdated, Sluggish, and Invisible
Eddie's Cut was operating on a legacy site builder. The platform was incredibly slow, aesthetically dated, and—worst of all—penalized heavily by Google for poor performance metrics. 

When a client wants to book a premium haircut, their first digital touchpoint needs to reflect that same level of quality. Instead, visitors were met with layout shifts, slow loading times, and a clunky booking integration. Furthermore, their business relied heavily on an established email ecosystem hosted on One.com, meaning any server migration came with the massive risk of dropping critical client communications.

### Our Solution: A Custom Next.js Architecture
We didn't just redesign their website; we ripped out the old foundation and built a mathematically perfect digital engine. 

1. **Next.js & React Ecosystem:** We migrated them off the sluggish site builder onto a custom Next.js architecture, utilizing static site generation for instantaneous page loads.
2. **Premium Micro-Interactions:** Using Framer Motion, we implemented an ultra-smooth, scroll-tracking navigation system that feels deeply satisfying to use, matching the bespoke nature of their services.
3. **Zero-Downtime DNS Migration:** We orchestrated a flawless server and DNS migration. We routed their new high-performance frontend through modern hosting while safely pointing their MX records back to One.com, ensuring their business emails never went offline for a single second.

### The Result: Pure Performance
The transformation was night and day. 

By stripping away the bloated legacy code, we achieved **perfect 100/100 Lighthouse Performance Scores** across Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, and SEO. 

The client now has a lightning-fast, highly responsive platform that instantly validates their premium pricing the second a visitor lands on the page. Eddie and his team are thrilled with the outcome—not just because the site looks beautiful, but because it finally runs like the top-tier business they have spent 25 years building.
