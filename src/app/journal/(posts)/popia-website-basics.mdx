The Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) applies to almost every South African business that collects personal information, and most websites do: a contact form collects a name, an email and a phone number.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For your specific situation, speak to a legal professional.

## 1. A privacy policy page

Explain in plain words what personal information your website collects, why, who you share it with (for example your email provider) and how people can ask you to see or delete it. Link it in the footer.

## 2. Contact forms that only ask for what you need

Only ask for what you really need to reply. Say what you will use it for, for example "We use your details only to reply to your request."

## 3. A cookie banner when you use tracking

If your website uses analytics or advertising cookies, tell visitors and give them a choice. Tools like Cookiebot handle this for you; we used it on the [Palmsure](/playroom/palmsure/) website.

## 4. Keep it secure

Use HTTPS (the padlock), keep your website software updated, and do not email sensitive information around in plain text.

## 5. Be careful with marketing emails

POPIA limits unsolicited electronic marketing. In general you need a person's consent, or they must be an existing customer, and every message needs an easy way to opt out.
