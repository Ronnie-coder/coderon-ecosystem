People choose an insurance broker on trust. Your website is often the first place they check whether you are real, authorised and easy to deal with. This checklist comes from redesigning the corporate website for [Palmsure Insurance](/playroom/palmsure/), which we still look after.

This is practical advice, not legal or compliance advice. Your compliance officer should sign off the content.

## 1. Your FSP number where people can see it

Show your authorised financial services provider number clearly, for example in the header or footer of every page.

## 2. The legal documents, easy to find

Publish the documents your compliance officer requires, such as your complaints policy, conflict of interest policy, privacy policy and any required disclosures. Put them on their own page, linked from the footer.

## 3. A page for each type of cover

Home and contents, vehicle, business: each with a short plain-English explanation, who it suits, and a clear "Get a quote" button.

## 4. A quote form that asks the right questions

A three-step form (what to insure, the details, how to reach you) gets you complete requests and fewer calls back and forth.

## 5. Your client portal one click away

If you or your insurers offer a client portal, put a clear "Client Portal" button in the main menu.

## 6. The insurers you work with

Showing the underwriters you place business with tells clients you can compare options for them.

## 7. Reviews

A Google review widget shows real client feedback without you having to copy it over by hand.

## 8. Cookie consent and a privacy policy

If your site uses analytics or tracking cookies, show a consent banner and a clear privacy policy, in line with POPIA.
