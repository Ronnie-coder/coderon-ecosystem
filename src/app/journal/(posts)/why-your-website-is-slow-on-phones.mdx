Most of your visitors are on a phone, often on mobile data. If your website takes more than a few seconds to show something useful, many of them leave. First, check where you stand: run your homepage through [Google PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/) and look at the mobile score.

## 1. Huge images

The most common cause. A photo straight from a camera can be several megabytes. **Fix:** resize images to the size they are shown and use modern formats like WebP. On [Palmsure](/playroom/palmsure/) we optimised every image for speed.

## 2. A heavy template or page builder

Some site builders and themes load a lot of code you never use. **Fix:** remove unused plugins, or rebuild on something lighter. When we redesigned [Eddie's Cut](/playroom/eddies-cut/), the mobile PageSpeed score went from 47 to 92.

## 3. Too many add-ons

Chat widgets, sliders, pop-ups, tracking tags: each one adds weight. **Fix:** keep the ones that earn their place, remove the rest.

## 4. Slow hosting

Cheap shared hosting can be slow to respond. **Fix:** better hosting or a content delivery network, so your site is served from close to the visitor.

## 5. Fonts and videos loading too early

Big background videos and many font files slow the first view. **Fix:** load them after the main content, or use lighter options.
