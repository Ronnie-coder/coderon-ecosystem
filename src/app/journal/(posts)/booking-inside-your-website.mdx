Many businesses use a booking system: Planway, Fresha, Booksy, Calendly, Setmore and others. They are good tools. The problem is how most websites connect to them.

## The problem: a button that sends people away

On many websites, the "Book now" button opens a completely different site. The visitor leaves your brand, lands somewhere that looks different, and sometimes gets distracted or closes the tab. You have done the hard work of getting them to your website, and then you hand them off.

## What we did for a salon in Denmark

[Eddie's Cut](/playroom/eddies-cut/), a hair salon in Hellerup, books through Planway, which is common in Denmark. On their old website the booking button took people off the site to Planway.

On the new website, the Planway booking opens in a window on the page itself. The client picks a treatment and a time without ever leaving Eddie's Cut. If the window ever has a problem, there is a small link to open the booking directly, so nobody gets stuck.

## How it works, simply

Most booking tools offer an "embed" option: a piece of code that shows the booking screen inside another website. We place it in a clean pop-up window that matches your design, works on phones, and closes with one tap.

## Is it always the best option?

Usually, yes. A few things to check first:

- Does your booking tool allow embedding? Most do.
- Is the embedded version fast on phones? We test it.
- Do you still want a direct link for your Google Business Profile and Instagram? Keep both.
