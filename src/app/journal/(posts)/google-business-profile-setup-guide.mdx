When someone searches "hairdresser near me" or "electrician Claremont", Google often shows a map with three businesses before any website. Those come from Google Business Profile. It is free, and for a local business it can matter as much as your website.

## Step 1: Create or claim your profile

Go to [google.com/business](https://www.google.com/business/) and search for your business name. If it already exists, claim it. If not, create it. Google will ask you to verify that you own the business, often with a video of your premises or a code.

## Step 2: Pick the right categories

Your **primary category** is the most important setting. Choose the one that describes your main service exactly ("Plumber", "Hair salon", "Tour operator"). Then add a few secondary categories for other things you really do.

## Step 3: Fill in everything

- Your business name, exactly as on your signage (no extra keywords)
- Phone number, website and opening hours
- Service areas, if you go to customers
- Services, using the same names as on your website

When we set up a profile for a salon, we copied their exact service names (cuts, colour, care, beard) from the website into the profile. Matching details help Google trust both.

## Step 4: Add real photos

Your shopfront, your team, your work. Add a few new ones every month.

## Step 5: Ask for reviews, every time

The best moment is right after a job goes well. Send a short WhatsApp with your review link. Reply to every review, good or bad, politely and briefly.

## Step 6: Link it to your website

Add your website link, and make sure your website shows the same name, address and phone number. If you take bookings online, add the booking link too.
