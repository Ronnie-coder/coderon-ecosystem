You search for your business on Google and... nothing. Or your website is on page five. Before you pay anyone, go through these checks. Most take a minute.

## 1. Google does not know your site exists yet

New websites can take days or weeks to appear. **Check:** type `site:yourbusiness.co.za` into Google. If nothing comes up, Google has not indexed your site.

**Fix:** set up free [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console), add your site and submit your sitemap.

## 2. Your site tells Google to stay away

A single setting, often left on by mistake after a redesign, tells search engines not to list the site (a "noindex" tag, or a robots.txt that blocks everything).

**Fix:** Search Console's URL Inspection tool shows if a page is blocked. In WordPress, check Settings → Reading → "Discourage search engines".

## 3. You are searching for the wrong thing

You will usually show up for your business name before anything else. Showing up for "plumber in Bellville" is harder, because many businesses compete for it.

**Fix:** make sure your site clearly says what you do and where: a page for each main service, with your town or suburb in the heading.

## 4. No Google Business Profile

For local searches, Google often shows the map results first. Those come from Google Business Profile, not your website.

**Fix:** create or claim your free [Google Business Profile](https://www.google.com/business/), add your website, hours, photos and service areas, and ask happy customers for reviews.

## 5. Missing titles and descriptions

The page title is the blue headline in Google results. If it just says "Home", Google has little to go on.

**Fix:** give every page a clear title, like "Geyser Repairs in Bellville | Smith Plumbing", and a one-sentence description.

## 6. The site is slow or broken on phones

Google mainly looks at the mobile version of your site. A slow, hard-to-use mobile site is a poor result to show people.

**Fix:** test your site with [Google PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/). A mobile score under 50 is worth fixing.

## 7. The site shows "Not secure"

If your site does not load over HTTPS, browsers warn visitors, and many leave right away.

**Fix:** your hosting provider can usually turn on a free SSL certificate in a few clicks.
