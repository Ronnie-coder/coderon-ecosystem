Most people who need a plumber or an electrician are standing in a wet kitchen or a dark house, holding a phone. They will not read much. They want to know three things fast: do you do this job, do you work in my area, and how do I reach you now?

Here is what your website needs to answer those questions.

## 1. A tap-to-call and WhatsApp button on every page

Put them at the top and keep them visible as people scroll. Nobody should have to hunt for your number.

## 2. The areas you work in, in plain sight

"Plumber in Bellville, Durbanville and Parow" tells a visitor (and Google) exactly where you work. If you cover many areas, give the main ones their own page.

## 3. One page per main service

Geyser repairs, burst pipes, drain unblocking, compliance certificates: each deserves its own page with a short description, a few photos and the questions people ask. These pages are what show up when someone searches for that exact job.

## 4. Proof you are real

Your registration (for example your PIRB or wireman's licence), a few real job photos, and Google reviews. People hire tradespeople they trust.

## 5. Speed on mobile data

A slow site loses people before they see your number. Test yours with [Google PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/): a mobile score below 50 is worth fixing.

## 6. Your Google Business Profile linked up

For "near me" searches, Google usually shows the map first. Make sure your profile has the same name, number and areas as your website, and links to it.

## 7. Clear prices, or at least a clear way to get one

You do not have to list every price, but a call-out fee or a "from" price builds trust. If not, make the quote request simple.
