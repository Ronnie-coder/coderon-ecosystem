There are two common ways to pay for a small business website in South Africa: once-off, or a monthly fee. Neither is always better. Here is how to choose.

## Once-off

You pay to have the website built, then pay separately for hosting and any changes later.

- **Good for:** businesses that can pay upfront and want the lowest cost over a few years.
- **Watch for:** who looks after updates, security and backups after launch.

Our once-off websites start at R3,500 for one page, R8,500 for up to 5 pages and R16,500 for up to 10 pages. Many clients add a care plan from R450 a month so someone keeps the site healthy.

## Monthly

You pay the first month to start, then one monthly fee covers the website, hosting, updates and small changes. You buy your own domain.

- **Good for:** new businesses, or anyone who would rather keep cash in the business.
- **Watch for:** the notice period, who owns the domain, and what happens to the site if you stop paying.

Our Monthly Website is R599 a month with hosting included. You buy your own domain (about R100 a year for a .co.za), pay the first month upfront, then go month to month with 30 days notice to cancel. Your domain is always yours; the website itself stays with us if you cancel.

## A quick comparison over two years

| | Once-off Starter + Care Basic | Monthly Website |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upfront | R3,500 | R599 (first month) |
| Per month after that | R450 | R599 |
| Total after 24 months | R14,300 | R14,376 |

The totals land close together. Both need a domain, but the once-off total does not include hosting, while the monthly one does. The real differences: with once-off you own the website, and with monthly you keep cash in the business and hosting is handled for you.
