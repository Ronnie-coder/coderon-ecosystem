There are two common ways to pay for a small business website in South Africa: once-off, or a monthly fee. Neither is always better. Here is how to choose.

## Once-off

You pay to have the website built, then pay separately for hosting and any changes later.

- **Good for:** businesses that can pay upfront and want the lowest cost over a few years.
- **Watch for:** who looks after updates, security and backups after launch.

Our once-off websites start at R3,500 for one page, R8,500 for up to 5 pages and R16,500 for up to 10 pages. Many clients add a care plan from R450 a month so someone keeps the site healthy.

## Monthly

You pay nothing upfront. One monthly fee covers the website, hosting, updates and small changes.

- **Good for:** new businesses, or anyone who would rather keep cash in the business.
- **Watch for:** the notice period, and what happens to the site if you stop paying.

Our Monthly Website is R599 a month, month to month, with 30 days notice to cancel.

## A quick comparison over two years

| | Once-off Starter + Care Basic | Monthly Website |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upfront | R3,500 | R0 |
| Per month | R450 | R599 |

The totals land close together. The real question is cash flow: do you want to pay now, or spread it out?
